Can you help if I live in York, Harrisburg, Reading, or another nearby county?

Yes. Remote Windows support works the same way from Lancaster County, York, Dauphin (Harrisburg), Lebanon, Berks (Reading), Chester, Cumberland, and Adams counties, as long as the PC can start and connect to the internet.

How does a remote support session work?

Call, text, or send the website form. We recommend the right service, then send a remote-support link. You approve the connection, stay in control, and can watch the work. Extra time is discussed and approved before any added charge.

Do you help small businesses as well as homes?

Yes. The same remote Windows help is available for homes and small businesses in the Lancaster area. Tell us what the computer is doing and we will recommend Basic support, a tune-up, malware cleanup, or a monthly care plan before you pay.
