Business Care

Tired of paying several companies? One monthly price. You approve every step.

If you are paying other people or other companies to handle repeat work, Business Care brings that work back onto accounts your business owns. We automate the parts your current tools can run. You own the accounts, and you decide what happens. Nothing goes live or gets sent until you approve it.

One monthly price covers the repeat work agreed for that business. It can include more than one task you are currently paying other people or companies to do, on the website or in the rest of the business. The monthly price covers the automations in the written plan. If that plan lists five and you add two more, the price goes up. The monthly price is set after that list is real, not before.

We look at the tools you already use first. Only repeat work you already pay someone else to do can be on the list, and only if those tools can run it. If a test draft does not work in those tools, that task is not part of your setup. One-off design, ads, and anything that needs its own paid license are out of scope.

Based in Manheim, Pennsylvania. The task list and the monthly price are written down with that business before the monthly work starts.

How it works

Talk, test, then a written plan.

STEP 01

Talk through the need

We talk through what the business needs, especially repeat work you already pay other people or companies to do, and which tools you already use.

STEP 02

Time for mock-ups

We take time to prepare mock-ups and a working test. A task is included in the written plan only after a test draft works in your current tools.

STEP 03

A written plan

We come back with a written plan: the list of tasks the monthly price covers, the monthly price, and how you approve work before it goes live or gets sent. The price is set after that list is real.

Adding another job you pay a vendor for is a change to that written list, and it can change the price. It is not an open door to every outside bill.
