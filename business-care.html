Business Care

A website your business owns.

Business Care starts with a website the business owns. One person sets up the repetitive work and keeps it running: inbox sorting, follow-ups, appointment reminders, and new inquiries landing in one place instead of getting lost across email and texts.

You own the accounts. Before a website change goes live, you see screenshots or a short screen recording. A preview link is included only when your own host already provides one. For inbox sorting, follow-ups, and reminders, you see the draft itself: the email or text that would be sent, or a short clip of the sort. Nothing goes live or gets sent until you approve it.

Based in Manheim, Pennsylvania. Business Care is arranged with that business, on the website and accounts the business owns.
