Coin Pals Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Your game stays on your device

Coin Pals does not collect or share user data during gameplay. Player profiles, game progress, earned game coins, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not sent to Roth Games Ltd.

About this policy

Roth Games Ltd. develops Coin Pals. This policy describes the privacy practices of the current offline Android game, together with information you may choose to provide when contacting us. Hosting this page on The Repair Nerds website does not change the developer responsible for Coin Pals.

Data collection and sharing

Coin Pals runs offline and does not transmit personal information, device identifiers, or gameplay activity to us or to third parties. The game has no online account registration, advertising, analytics, automatic crash reporting, or purchases with real money. Local player profiles are not online accounts.

We do not sell user data or use gameplay information for advertising or tracking.

Local saves and how to delete them

The game stores local information needed to remember your profiles, progress, rewards, unlocks, and settings. This information remains on your device for your use of the game. We do not have a server copy of it.

You can remove local app data through Android Settings by opening the Coin Pals app information screen, selecting Storage, and choosing Clear data or Clear storage. The exact labels depend on your device. This resets your saved progress and settings. Uninstalling the app also normally removes its local app data.

Depending on your device settings and the app's backup configuration, Android backup or device transfer services may preserve app data. These services are controlled by your device and backup provider. Roth Games Ltd. does not receive or access these backups. Manage any backup copies through the relevant service's settings.

Contacting us voluntarily

If you email us for support or with a privacy question, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. This is separate from automatic data collection by the game. Please avoid sending passwords, financial information, or other sensitive details.

We use your message to respond and resolve your request. Our email provider, Google/Gmail, processes these communications to deliver and store them. We do not sell your correspondence or share it for advertising. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

We limit access to support correspondence to those who need it to handle your request and use the email provider's security protections. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us using the contact below, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children's privacy

The game's no-collection approach applies to all players, including children. Coin Pals does not ask players to submit personal information to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support correspondence, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate.

This webpage and external services

Opening this policy or an external link in a browser is separate from offline gameplay. This policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the webpage under its own privacy statement. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels.

Google Play and your device's operating system may also process information independently under their own policies and your settings. This policy describes Roth Games Ltd.'s practices for Coin Pals.

Changes to this policy

If the game's features or privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date, along with the relevant Google Play disclosures. Any required notices or consent will be provided before introducing data practices that require them.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Roth Games Ltd. at RothGamesLtd@gmail.com.
