Protect yourself

Do not trust a phone number just because it appears in a pop-up.

Unexpected warnings often try to frighten people into calling quickly. Close or step away from the message and contact someone using a website or number you already recognize. The Repair Nerds' published number is 717-535-6525.

Never place passwords, banking information, Social Security numbers, or security codes in the website support form or an email.

Questions

Senior computer-help FAQ

What if I am not good with computers?

That is completely fine. Describe what you see on the screen, and we will guide you one step at a time using plain language without rushing.

How do I allow remote computer help?

After you contact us, you receive a support link and instructions. You approve the connection before the technician can see or control the computer, and you can watch the session.

Can a family member help coordinate the appointment?

A trusted family member or caregiver can help communicate and coordinate with the computer owner's knowledge and permission. The person using the computer must still approve the remote connection.

Do I have to choose a service before calling?

No. Tell us what is happening, and we will help identify the appropriate service and price before payment and repair work.

Do you work on Apple or Chromebook computers?

The Repair Nerds currently provides remote support for Windows PCs only.
